Yummle v1.3.6 ships one small quality-of-life feature: videos can now be deleted directly from the thumbnail grids in the library, channel, and playlist views, so reclaiming disk space no longer requires a detour through each video's watch page. No backend, API, or data changes.

## Added

- **Delete button on video thumbnails.** Cards with downloaded media in the library, channel, and playlist grids now show a trash-icon overlay on the thumbnail (revealed on hover on desktop, always visible on touch devices). Confirming the dialog removes the local media file exactly like the watch page's "Delete video" button — metadata, thumbnails, comments, and catalog information stay, the video becomes catalog-only and re-downloadable, and the card reflects that immediately without a page reload. Catalog-only cards show no overlay, search-result tiles are untouched (a separate layout, out of scope here), and the catalog-download button behaves as before.

## Notes

- **Deletion is confirmed and guarded.** The overlay asks for confirmation before calling the existing delete-media API, reports success or failure inline above the grid, and ignores extra clicks while a delete is in flight.
- **No API or data changes.** The feature reuses `DELETE /api/videos/{id}/media`; endpoints, storage, and stored rows are unchanged, and the watch-page delete button works exactly as before.

No upgrade steps: nothing to migrate, and nothing changes unless you click the new delete control.
